# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Being respectful of differing opinions and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility for our mistakes and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for people using the project

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission

## Something specific to this project

This repo ships a **deliberately vulnerable** MCP server and rules for finding
the same class of bug in real software. Two things follow from that:

- Do not use the lab, its exploits, or the detection rules against systems you
  do not own or have written permission to test.
- If you report a flaw in someone else's software that you found with these
  rules, follow that project's disclosure process. Do not post a working
  exploit against live third-party software in this repo's issues.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to **`winstonrgsocial@gmail.com`**. Reports are
reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the privacy and security of
the reporter is respected.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith may
face temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by the project's
leadership.
